\advexercise{$R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}$ versus $V_{GS}$}{\texttt{.step}, \texttt{.meas}}

\subsubsection*{Objective}
Find how the ON-resistance of a MOSFET switch depends on the gate drive, and automate the measurement with
\texttt{.step} and \texttt{.meas}. Compared with Exercise~5.8, which uses one gate voltage, this exercise requires a parameter
sweep, an automatic measurement and the comparison of an exact model with an approximation.

\subsubsection*{Task}
\task{The MOSFET of Exercise~5.8 drives the same $100\,\Omega$ load from $V_{DD} = 12$\,V, but the gate is now driven by a DC
source $V_G$. Measure $R_{DS(\mathrm{on})} = V_{DS}/I_D$ for $V_G$ = 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 10\,V, plot it versus $V_G$ and compare it
with the small-$V_{DS}$ approximation $R_{DS(\mathrm{on})} \approx 1/[K_P(V_{GS}-V_{GS(\mathrm{Th})})]$.}
\circuitfig{adv01}{Circuit of Advanced Exercise 5.A1}
\components{
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
  \item $V_{DD} = 12$\,V, $R_L = 100\,\Omega$, $V_G$ = \texttt{\{VG\}} (gate to ground).
  \item M1: \texttt{.model SW NMOS(Level=1 Vto=2 Kp=0.5 Lambda=0)}, $L = W = 1\,\mu$m.
\end{itemize}}

\subsubsection*{Simulation}
\begin{spice}
.model SW NMOS(Level=1 Vto=2 Kp=0.5 Lambda=0)
.param VG=5
.step param VG list 3 4 5 6 8 10
.op
.meas OP Rds FIND V(d)/Id(M1)
.meas OP Pcond FIND V(d)*Id(M1)
\end{spice}
Run, press \textit{A} and plot \texttt{V(d)/Id(M1)} ($R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}$) and \texttt{V(d)*Id(M1)} (loss) versus $V_G$; use the cursors to read the values. The \texttt{.meas} lines report only the first step ($V_G=3$\,V): $R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}=2.314\,\Omega$, $P=31.83$\,mW.
\simfig{adv01_sim}{$R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}$ (bottom) and conduction loss (top) versus $V_G$; cursors at $V_G=4$\,V (14.55\,mW) and $5$\,V (9.60\,mW)}

\subsubsection*{Questions}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item Fill in a table of $V_G$, $R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}$ (approximation), $R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}$ (simulation) and the conduction
        loss $P = V_{DS} I_D$. For which $V_G$ is the approximation worst, and why?
  \item The input of Exercise~5.8 gives $V_{GS} = 4.55$\,V. Would a 3.3\,V logic output be acceptable to drive this MOSFET? Justify with
        the measured $R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}$ and power loss.
  \item What happens to $R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}$ if \texttt{Kp} is doubled? Check with a second \texttt{.step}.
\end{enumerate}
\answerspace{8}
\begin{solution}
Exact values from the triode equation $I_D = K_P[(V_{GS}-2)V_{DS} - V_{DS}^2/2]$ with $I_D = (12-V_{DS})/100$:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c c c c c}
\toprule
$V_G$ (V) & $R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}$ approx. ($\Omega$) & $R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}$ exact ($\Omega$) & $I_D$ (mA) & $P$ (mW)\\ \midrule
3 & 2.00 & 2.31 & 117.3 & 31.8\\
4 & 1.00 & 1.03 & 118.8 & 14.6\\
5 & 0.667 & 0.676 & 119.2 & 9.6\\
6 & 0.500 & 0.504 & 119.4 & 7.2\\
8 & 0.333 & 0.334 & 119.6 & 4.8\\
10 & 0.250 & 0.250 & 119.7 & 3.6\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
(1) The approximation neglects the $V_{DS}^2/2$ term; it is worst at $V_G = 3$\,V, where $V_{DS}$ is no longer small compared
with $V_{GS}-V_{GS(\mathrm{Th})} = 1$\,V. (2) With 3.3\,V the overdrive is only 1.3\,V: $R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}$ rises to
about 1.6--1.7\,$\Omega$ and the loss is more than twice that at 5\,V; it still works for this load but the margin is small and a
real logic-level MOSFET would be chosen. (3) Doubling \texttt{Kp} halves $R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}$.
The simulation confirms the table: $R_{DS(\mathrm{on})}=2.314\,\Omega$ and $P=31.83$\,mW at $V_G=3$\,V, $P=14.55$\,mW at 4\,V and $9.60$\,mW at 5\,V.
\end{solution}
