\section{Introduction}

In a bipolar transistor the collector current is controlled by the base \emph{current}. A field-effect transistor (FET)
is controlled by a \emph{voltage}: the gate--source voltage $V_{GS}$ sets the width of a conducting channel between drain
and source, and the gate draws practically no current. This very high input resistance makes FETs the basic element
of digital ICs, analog switches and power switches.

This lab, adapted from Chapter~6 of the original PSpice manual, covers the three main FET families:
the junction FET (JFET), the depletion-type MOSFET (D-MOSFET) and the enhancement-type MOSFET (E-MOSFET).
You first measure their characteristic curves and parameters ($I_{DSS}$, $V_P$, $V_{GS(\mathrm{Th})}$, $k$), then
bias them in the configurations studied in class, and finally use a MOSFET as a switch for resistive and inductive loads.
Every device is described by a short \texttt{.model} statement, so the simulation can be compared exactly with the
square-law equations used in the theory:
\[
  I_D = I_{DSS}\left(1-\frac{V_{GS}}{V_P}\right)^2 \quad\text{(JFET, D-MOSFET)},\qquad
  I_D = k\left(V_{GS}-V_{GS(\mathrm{Th})}\right)^2 \quad\text{(E-MOSFET)}.
\]

\subsection{Objectives}
After this lab, students will be able to:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item Plot the transfer and output characteristics of a JFET and read $I_{DSS}$ and $V_P$.
  \item Calculate and simulate self-bias and voltage-divider bias of a JFET.
  \item Define depletion-type and enhancement-type MOSFETs with a \texttt{.model} statement and bias them.
  \item Use a MOSFET as a switch, protect it when driving an inductive load, and build a bidirectional driver.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Analysis types introduced}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Operating point} (\texttt{.op}) -- bias circuits (Exercises 5.3--5.5, 5.7).
  \item \textbf{DC sweep} and \textbf{nested DC sweep} (\texttt{.dc} with one or two sources) -- characteristic curves (5.1, 5.2, 5.5, 5.6).
  \item \textbf{Transient analysis} (\texttt{.tran}) with a \texttt{PULSE} source -- switching circuits (5.8--5.10).
  \item \textbf{User-defined device models} (\texttt{.model} for \texttt{NJF}, \texttt{NMOS}, \texttt{PMOS}).
  \item Advanced exercises: \textbf{parameter sweep} (\texttt{.step}) and \textbf{measurements} (\texttt{.meas}).
\end{itemize}
