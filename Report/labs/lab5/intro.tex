\subsection*{Objectives}
After this lab, students will be able to:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item Plot transfer and output characteristics of JFETs and MOSFETs.
  \item Design self-bias and voltage-divider bias of a JFET.
  \item Define a depletion-type MOSFET with a \texttt{.model} statement.
  \item Use a MOSFET as a switch and to drive an inductive load.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Analysis types introduced}
DC sweep and nested DC sweep (\texttt{.dc}), \texttt{.op}, \texttt{.tran}, user-defined \texttt{.model}.
