\subsection{Objectives}
After this lab, students will be able to:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item Plot the transfer and output characteristics of a JFET and read $I_{DSS}$ and $V_P$.
  \item Calculate and simulate self-bias and voltage-divider bias of a JFET.
  \item Define depletion-type and enhancement-type MOSFETs with a \texttt{.model} statement and bias them.
  \item Use a MOSFET as a switch, protect it when driving an inductive load, and build a bidirectional driver.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Analysis types introduced}
DC sweep and nested DC sweep (\texttt{.dc}), \texttt{.op}, \texttt{.tran}, user-defined \texttt{.model}.
