\subsection{Objectives}
After this lab, students will be able to:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item Plot the V--I characteristic of a diode with a DC sweep.
  \item Compare the LTspice default diode model, a real diode model and the 0.7~V constant-drop model.
  \item Simulate clamper, rectifier, Zener regulator and AC/DC power-supply circuits in the time domain.
  \item Understand the effect of the maximum time step on transient results.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Analysis types introduced}
DC sweep (\texttt{.dc}), transient (\texttt{.tran}), \texttt{.meas}, \texttt{.step}.
