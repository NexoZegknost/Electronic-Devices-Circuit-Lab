\subsection{Objectives}
After this lab, students will be able to:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item Identify the cut-off, active and saturation regions of a BJT.
  \item Plot BJT output characteristics with a nested DC sweep.
  \item Use BJTs as switches, current mirrors, Darlington pairs and logic gates.
  \item Model an optocoupler in LTspice.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Analysis types introduced}
\texttt{.op}, nested DC sweep (\texttt{.dc} with two sources), \texttt{.tran}, \texttt{.step}.
